Polymarket Edge Hunt

15+ strategy families tested with walk-forward backtests on real trade prints, an independent audit, leaderboard forensics of 5,192 wallets and live paper monitors. This page reports what held up once fees, fills and time were accounted for.

Bottom line

I did not find a strategy that meets all of your conditions at once (≤$100 start, ≥$100/day, fills identical to the test, no speed, no decay). Every edge that survived out-of-sample testing is either small in capacity or needs a data-speed advantage. The results in the table are what the evidence supports.

  • Two small, non-speed edges survived out-of-sample: tweet-count markets for small X accounts (+18.6% per trade, t 2.3, ≈$10/day) and selling daily crypto barrier longshots (BTC +0.54% per trade with realistic fills, both halves positive, ≈$40–50/day at full capacity).
  • The biggest real money for small accounts is in weather observation-day trading, but it is a 60–120-second data race. Public feeds arrive after the edge is gone.
  • Liquidity-reward farming is real income, but small uninformed farmers net a median ≈+0.25%/day and lose in about 1 month out of 3.
  • Hard blocker: this research machine is geoblocked (US). Live trading must run from a jurisdiction where Polymarket allows it, on your own account.

Weather markets

Weather is the largest pool that small, consistent wallets actually win in. About 51 daily-high events trade ≈$2.6M a day, and 45% of the money trades on the observation day between 11:00 and 18:00 local time.

The station-reading race

Once a METAR shows a temperature above a bucket, that bucket is effectively dead. The METAR matches the resolution in 99.6–99.8% of days. Across 40,228 such deaths, cheap NO shares traded mostly in the first 15–90 seconds after the observation time. The public aviationweather.gov feed publishes a median 274 s after the observation, when NO is already at 0.998.

The top wallets doing this (Weatherstappen, bhuumi) trade 40–200 s after the observation. They have faster feeds than the public one. A live probe of the two public feeds measured the same thing: aviationweather.gov delivered METARs ≈8 minutes after observation and the NWS API ≈20 minutes after. My own 2-minute live monitor saw zero dead buckets below 0.999 today.

Forecast and intraday models

A walk-forward model built on ECMWF open data and the US National Blend of Models was less accurate than the market (bucket log-loss 0.252 vs 0.220 the day before) and added nothing when stacked with the price. An intraday model using the forecast, the running maximum and the hour of day was also beaten by the market at every hour. In weather, slow public data is already in the price. The money is in being first with the reading.

How Jev and the LLM are used

Jev (TypeSafe jev-1.13) returns calibrated probabilities for typed questions. It has no web access. Asked about outcomes with no evidence, it had no forecasting skill (AUC 0.61–0.63), and it had no knowledge of 2026 outcomes either, so it does not contaminate backtests. As a verification gate it was perfect on 300 station/unit checks. The bot runs it on every new market before trading, for about $0.00002 a check.

The LLM side does the research. A research agent gathers dated evidence per market (box-office trackers, leaderboards, consensus figures). Jev then judges the evidence against the market's rules. This matches how the most durable small wallets work (Started-with-20-USD: $20 in January, +$55k by September; Quarrelsome-Branch: 93% of 95 weeks profitable). A backtest can't measure this honestly, because web search leaks outcomes. So it is running as a forward test on 27 open markets that resolve 27–30 September.

What starting with $100 realistically looks like

The two adopted edges together stake about $10–12k a day and earn roughly $50–60 a day. Barrier positions settle within 12 hours, so about $10k of capital covers it, and a single loss costs a full stake. From $100, the same rules earn under $1 a day. No tested strategy turns $100 into $100 a day. The wallets that do make $100–300 a day on about $1k either win the weather data race with faster feeds, or research niche markets by hand every day.

If you want to chase the larger number, there are two routes, and neither is proven. One is to buy a faster observation feed and compete in the weather race; the backtest says $250–540 a day at 60–120 s, with a thinning margin. The other is to automate niche research with the LLM + Jev pipeline, which only the forward test can validate.

Run it

Everything is on branch claude/nice-fermi-75y27f. The bot defaults to paper mode and refuses to trade live from a geoblocked IP.

pip install polymarket-client requests pandas timezonefinder
# paper mode (default): simulated fills against live order books
PAPER=1 python3 bot/strategies/btc_barrier_no.py      # adopted: sell daily BTC barrier longshots
python3 src/llm_pipeline/research.py 20              # LLM research + Jev judge (OpenRouter key in .env)
python3 bot/settle.py                                # mark paper fills to resolution
# live: only from a jurisdiction Polymarket allows, with your own account
PAPER=0 POLYMARKET_PRIVATE_KEY=... POLYMARKET_WALLET_ADDRESS=... python3 bot/strategies/btc_barrier_no.py
